Draw: drag around a visible bumper. Select: drag a box to move it, or drag a corner to resize. Change its color above; Delete removes the selected box.

Only checked frames enter the YOLO export. Model suggestions and edits remain drafts until you check the frame. An empty frame can be checked if no bumper is visible.
Export folder

Checked frames save clean photos under images/train/ and matching boxes under labels/train/. Originals remain unchanged. No boxed-image copies, upload, or training is started here.
