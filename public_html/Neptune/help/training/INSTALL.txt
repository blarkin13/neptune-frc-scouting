NEPTUNE TRAINING CENTER / CBT
================================

Installed URLs:
  /help/training/                  Training Center
  /help/training/cbt.php?course=admin
  /help/training/cbt.php?course=scouter
  /help/training/records.php       Owner/Admin organization training records
  /help/e/                         Redirect to Training Center
  /help/event-training.php         Event Operations & Scouting manual

CBT rules:
  - 20 randomized questions per attempt from a larger question pool
  - answer order randomized
  - 85% passing score
  - retakes allowed
  - every completed attempt and grade retained
  - Admin CBT: owner/admin roles
  - Scouter CBT: all logged-in roles
  - Records: owner/admin, organization-scoped

Database:
  The Training Center creates training_attempts automatically with CREATE TABLE IF NOT EXISTS.
  sql/Neptune_Training_CBT.sql is also included as a manual/clean-install migration.
  The Maintenance Console copies SQL files but does not execute them; normally no manual SQL step is needed because the Training Center self-initializes.

Navigation changes:
  - Command Center receives a Training & Certification card.
  - Match Scouting receives a Training Center card.
  - Match Control/Match Scouting wording recognizes TBA OR verified manual schedules.

After installation:
  1. Open /help/training/
  2. Confirm the page loads and the database table initializes.
  3. Take one Scouter CBT and submit it.
  4. As Owner/Admin open /help/training/records.php and verify the grade is listed.
  5. Open Command Center and Match Scouting and verify the new Training cards.
